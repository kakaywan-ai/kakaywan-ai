# DEMO ONLY Fictional Eco Guide

## Waste segregation (fictional)

In this fictional guide, households in the imaginary Barangay Pine Hollow separate waste into three bins: green for biodegradable waste, blue for recyclable waste, and grey for residual waste. Biodegradable waste is collected on Mondays and recyclable waste on Thursdays.

## Tree planting day (fictional)

The imaginary Pine Hollow Tree Planting Day takes place every first Saturday of July. Volunteers must register at the fictional Barangay Hall and bring a reusable water bottle. Seedlings are provided by the fictional Green Office.

## Park visitor rules (fictional)

At the imaginary Cloud Meadow Park, visitors must keep to marked trails, must not pick flowers, and must carry their trash out with them. Drones are not allowed in the park.
